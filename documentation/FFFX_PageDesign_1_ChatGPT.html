From what we've established so far, Form follows f(x) (FFFX) occupies a very different role from Cabinet or Bookshelf.

  • Cabinet = the map of your work.

  • Bookshelf = a curated archive of reading and writing.

  • FFFX = the laboratory. A place where ideas become systems, algorithms, geometry, materials, simulations, tools and computational objects.

Rather than showcasing finished projects, it should feel like you're wandering through a designer's sketchbook crossed with a mathematics notebook and a fabrication lab.

A generated concept for the page

Picture the browser opening onto an almost-white canvas with faint construction lines, as though you're looking at an architect's drafting table.

Centered near the top:

FORM FOLLOWS f(x)

geometry • computation • fabrication • emergence
